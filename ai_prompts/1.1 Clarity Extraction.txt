CLARITY EXTRACTION ARCHITECTURE

Raw thoughts:
[Stream of consciousness]

Step 1: Separate Content
Identify and separate:
- Emotional content
- Analytical content
- Factual claims
- Assumptions

Step 2: Group Cognitive Threads
Organize thoughts into distinct threads.
Label each clearly.

Step 3: Extract Questions
Identify:
- Explicit questions
- Implicit questions
- Hidden fears
- Unstated desired outcomes

Step 4: Identify Tensions
- Internal contradictions
- Value conflicts
- Goal misalignments

Step 5: Classify Issue Type
Is this primarily:
- Binary decision
- Trade-off optimization
- Priority overload
- Identity conflict
- Information gap
- Emotional regulation issue

Step 6: Surface Constraints
Identify constraints that are:
- Real
- Assumed
- Socially imposed
- Self-imposed

Step 7: Signal vs Noise
Label which elements are:
- High signal
- Low signal
- Repetitive
- Emotionally amplified

Step 8: Distill Core Question
Reduce everything to:
- One central problem statement
- One core decision or uncertainty

Deliver:
- Structured outline
- Bullet summary
- One-sentence core question
- Recommended next tool from the system to apply
