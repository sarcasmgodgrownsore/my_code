MEETING PREPARATION FRAMEWORK

Meeting topic: [topic]
Attendees: [roles / titles]
Meeting type:
[Informational / Decision-making / Negotiation / Performance review / Alignment / Conflict resolution]

My objective in this meeting:
[Secure approval / Clarify direction / Defend position / Influence decision / Gather intelligence]

Context:
[Relevant background]

1) Stakeholder Mapping
For each attendee:
- Likely priorities
- Incentives
- Risk tolerance
- Influence level

2) Likely Questions They May Ask Me

3) Questions I Should Ask Them

4) Core Message Framing
- One-sentence position statement
- Supporting rationale
- Evidence/data required
- Tone to project

5) Likely Objections
For each:
- What they might say
- Why
- Recommended response strategy

6) Political & Emotional Risk Scan
- Sensitive topics
- Language to avoid
- Potential triggers

7) Risks That Could Derail the Meeting

8) Definition of Success
What outcome must be achieved?

9) Preparation Checklist
Before the meeting, ensure:
- Key data prepared
- Clear examples ready
- Opening statement rehearsed
- Closing ask clarified
