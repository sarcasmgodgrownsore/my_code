LEARNING THROUGH QUESTIONS INSTEAD OF PASSIVE EXPLANATION

Topic: [topic]

Target outcome:
By the end, I should be able to:
[Define / Apply / Derive / Compare / Prove / Explain intuitively / Solve problems involving X]

Session depth:
[Quick diagnostic / Standard lesson / Deep mastery session]

Teach me through guided questioning instead of direct explanation.

Process:
1) Start by asking what I currently understand.
2) Ask one focused question at a time.
3) Progress through stages:
   - Concept recognition
   - Mechanism understanding
   - Application
   - Edge cases / abstraction
4) When I answer:
   - Identify precise misunderstandings.
   - Correct them briefly.
   - Ask a follow-up question to repair the gap.
5) At checkpoints, ask me to summarize what I understand and what remains unclear.
6) Gradually increase difficulty.
7) Do not provide long lectures unless necessary for clarification.

If the topic is mathematical or technical:
- Use formal notation when appropriate.
- Ask derivation or proof-style questions.
- Include counterexamples when useful.
