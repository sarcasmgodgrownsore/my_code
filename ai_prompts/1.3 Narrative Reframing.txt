STRATEGIC NARRATIVE REFRAMING ARCHITECTURE

Current narrative:
[Describe how I am framing this situation]

Step 1: Deconstruct the Narrative
- Protagonist (How am I positioned?)
- Antagonist (What is framed as the threat?)
- Stakes (What feels at risk?)
- Implied future trajectory
- Emotional tone

Step 2: Separate Structure
Distinguish:
- Verifiable facts
- Interpretations
- Predictions
- Emotional conclusions

Step 3: Identify Hidden Assumptions

Step 4: Offer Alternative Framings Across Distinct Lenses:
- Strategic lens (long-term optimization)
- Learning lens (skill acquisition)
- Identity lens (who I am becoming)
- Systems lens (structural forces at play)
- Probabilistic lens (uncertainty realism)

For each alternative framing:
- Core reframed narrative
- How it changes risk perception
- How it changes opportunity perception
- How it changes emotional tone
- Strengths
- Risks or distortions introduced
- Identity reinforced
- Identity weakened

Step 5: Compare Framings
- Most comfortable
- Most honest
- Most strategically useful

Step 6: Stress-Test the Recommended Framing
- What would falsify it?
- Under what conditions would it fail?

Recommend the framing that optimizes long-term agency, clarity, and strategic positioning — not just emotional comfort.
