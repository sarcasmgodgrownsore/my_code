DEVIL’S ADVOCATE AND RISK EXPOSURE

I am considering this decision: [decision]

Context:
- Time horizon:
- Scale of impact:
- Stakeholders involved:
- Reversibility (easy / moderate / hard to undo):

Your role: Rigorous critic.

Argue against this decision.

For each major risk:
- Describe the risk clearly.
- Estimate likelihood (low / medium / high).
- Estimate impact severity.
- Identify underlying assumptions being violated.

Include:
- Hidden and non-obvious risks.
- Operational, financial, strategic, reputational risks.
- Second- and third-order consequences.
- Psychological biases influencing this decision.
- Where this commonly fails in real-world cases.

Run a pre-mortem:
Assume this decision failed catastrophically in 2 years.
Explain the most likely cause.

Optional:
- Under what conditions could this succeed?
- What mitigation strategies reduce highest-impact risks?

Do not soften the critique.
Be specific and grounded.
