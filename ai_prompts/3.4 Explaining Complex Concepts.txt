EXPLAINING COMPLEX CONCEPTS AT MULTIPLE DEPTHS

Explain [concept] at three levels.

Keep the core definition consistent across all levels.
Only increase depth — do not change the meaning.

Level 1: No Background
- Define the concept in plain language.
- Explain how it works using simple terms.
- Provide a relatable example or analogy.

Level 2: Foundational Understanding
- Provide a more precise definition.
- Explain the underlying mechanism.
- Describe practical applications or implications.

Level 3: Advanced Understanding
Assume I already understand core theory and terminology.
- Explore edge cases and limitations.
- Discuss trade-offs and failure modes.
- Clarify subtle distinctions.
- Compare with closely related concepts.
- Identify common advanced misunderstandings.

Depth mode: [Concise / Standard / Deep dive]

If the concept is technical or mathematical, emphasize formal structure and causal mechanisms.
