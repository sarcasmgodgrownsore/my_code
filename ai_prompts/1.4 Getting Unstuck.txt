GETTING UNSTUCK THROUGH DIAGNOSTIC THINKING

I am trying to achieve: [goal]
I am currently stuck on: [problem]

Diagnosis mode:
[Tactical / Strategic / Mixed]

Do not provide solutions yet.

First, ask 5–8 structured diagnostic questions.
Group them under:

1) Goal clarity
2) Assumptions
3) Constraints (classify as structural, psychological, resource-based, or political)
4) Information gaps
5) Bottleneck identification

Aim to uncover root causes rather than surface symptoms.
Do not provide advice, suggestions, or solutions yet.

After I respond:
1) Summarize your understanding of the real problem.
2) Identify likely root cause(s).
3) Offer 2–3 alternative hypotheses if relevant.
4) Then provide recommended next steps.
5) Identify potential cognitive biases influencing my framing.
