EXPLAINING COMPLEX CONCEPTS AT MULTIPLE DEPTHS

Explain [concept] at three levels:

Level 1: Assume I have no background knowledge.
Use simple language and relatable examples.

Level 2: Assume I understand the fundamentals.
Focus on mechanisms and practical applications.

Level 3: Assume I am a nearly expert.
Explain edge cases, trade-offs, limitations, and nuanced insights.

Keep each level clearly separated and progressively more technical.
