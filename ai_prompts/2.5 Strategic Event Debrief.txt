STRATEGIC POST-EVENT DEBRIEF ARCHITECTURE

Event:
[Meeting / Decision / Project]

Step 1: Objective Summary
- What happened?
- Outcome (good / neutral / poor)

Step 2: Outcome vs Process
- Outcome quality
- Decision/process quality
- Was this good execution or good luck?

Step 3: Expectation vs Reality
- What did I expect?
- What actually happened?
- Where did divergence occur?

Step 4: Control Analysis
- Factors within my control
- External factors
- Structural constraints

Step 5: Emotional Analysis
- Dominant emotions during event
- Emotional regulation quality
- Emotional residue afterward

Step 6: Bias Detection
Identify visible distortions:
- Confirmation bias
- Overconfidence
- Defensive reasoning
- Catastrophizing
- Sunk cost

Step 7: Signal vs Noise
- What was true signal?
- What was variance or randomness?

Step 8: Performance Scoring (1–10)
- Preparation
- Clarity
- Adaptability
- Emotional control
- Strategic positioning

Step 9: Lessons
- What worked
- What failed
- Root causes

Step 10: Forward Integration
- One behavior to repeat
- One behavior to stop
- One structural safeguard
- One skill to improve

Step 11: Identity Reflection
How did this event reinforce or challenge the person I am becoming?

Deliver:
- 3 key lessons
- 1 high-leverage change for next time
