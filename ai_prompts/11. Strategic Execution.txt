STRATEGIC EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

Goal:
[Clear objective]

Desired outcome:
- Observable result:
- Measurable criteria:
- Target timeframe:

Constraints:
[List all constraints]

Classify constraints:
- Hard (non-negotiable)
- Soft (flexible)
- Self-imposed vs external
- Identify the most binding constraint

Break the goal into:

1) Milestones (in logical sequence)

For each milestone:
- Deliverable
- Time allocation estimate
- Energy intensity (low / medium / high)
- Required skills or capability gaps
- Primary failure mode
- Secondary failure mode
- Recovery strategy

2) Critical path dependencies

3) Smallest Viable Progress
What is the minimum meaningful version achievable in 30 days?

4) Resource Allocation
- Time per week available
- Budget (if relevant)
- Support required

5) Metrics
- Leading indicators
- Lagging indicators

6) Feedback Cadence
- Weekly review questions
- Monthly checkpoint
- Pivot triggers

7) Decision Gates
Where must continuation, scaling, or abandonment be reassessed?

8) Friction & Momentum Analysis
- Where will motivation drop?
- Where will friction increase?
- How can activation energy be reduced?

9) First 7-Day Action Plan
Concrete, low-ambiguity actions only.

Be realistic.
Optimize for sustainability, not heroics.
Assume imperfect conditions.
