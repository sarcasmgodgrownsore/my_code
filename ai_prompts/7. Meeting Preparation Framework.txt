MEETING PREPARATION FRAMEWORK

I have a meeting about: [topic].
With: [roles / titles of attendees].
Context: [background if relevant].

Provide:
- Likely questions they may ask me.
- Questions I should ask them.
- Key points I must communicate clearly.
- Possible objections or friction points.
- Risks that could derail the meeting.
- Preparation checklist before the meeting.

Structure this in clear sections.
