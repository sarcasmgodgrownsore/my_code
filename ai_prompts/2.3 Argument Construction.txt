STRATEGIC ARGUMENT CONSTRUCTION ARCHITECTURE

Position:
[Claim]

Desired outcome:
[Inform / Influence / Secure approval / Shift perception / Defend position / Escalate]

Audience:
- Role:
- Priorities:
- Incentives:
- Fears:
- Decision authority:

Desired tone:
[Analytical / Assertive / Calm / Diplomatic / Urgent]

Desired audience emotional response:
[Confidence / Concern / Alignment / Urgency / Reassurance]

Burden of proof:
What must be demonstrated for this to be convincing?

Build the argument using:

1) Core Thesis (1 sentence)

2) Structured Reasoning
For each pillar:
- Claim
- Reason
- Evidence
- Implication

3) Strongest Counterargument
- Present it fairly
- Neutralize it

4) Assumption Check
- Identify weak assumptions
- Tighten claim if necessary

5) Framing Strategy
Select framing aligned with audience priorities:
- Efficiency
- Risk reduction
- Fairness
- Identity alignment
- Long-term gain
- Avoided loss

6) Overreach Detection
Where might this argument overstate?

Deliver:
- Executive summary (≤100 words)
- Structured full argument
