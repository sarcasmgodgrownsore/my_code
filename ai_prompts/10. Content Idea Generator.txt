CONTENT IDEA GENERATOR WITH DEPTH CONTROL

I create content about: [topic].
My audience is: [audience description].
My goal is: [growth / authority / conversions / engagement].

Generate 10 content ideas:
- First 5: Practical and immediately useful.
- Last 5: Unexpected angles, contrarian takes, or under-discussed perspectives.

For each idea include:
- Working headline.
- Core thesis.
- One key insight that makes it distinctive.

Avoid generic topics.
