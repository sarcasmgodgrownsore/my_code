COGNITIVE BIAS AUDIT ARCHITECTURE

Situation:
[Describe decision, belief, or emotional reaction]

Step 1: Emotional Mapping
- Dominant emotions
- Intensity (low / medium / high)
- How emotion may be shaping interpretation

Step 2: Identity Mapping
- What identity aspect feels threatened or reinforced?
- What belief about myself is being protected?

Step 3: Bias Classification
Identify potential distortions in these categories:
- Probability distortions
- Confirmation bias
- Incentive distortions
- Social/status distortions
- Identity-protective cognition
- Sunk cost or escalation patterns

For each identified bias:
- How it may be distorting perception
- Whether it is irrational distortion or adaptive heuristic
- Severity (low / medium / high)
- Potential impact on decision quality

Step 4: Probability Calibration
Where might I be:
- Overestimating risk?
- Underestimating risk?
- Treating low-probability events as inevitable?

Step 5: Social Influence Scan
- How expectations or reputation concerns may distort judgment

Step 6: Counterweights
For high-impact biases:
- Behavioral correction
- Information to seek
- Questions to ask
- Structural safeguards

Step 7: Overcorrection Warning
Where might correcting too aggressively create new distortion?

Deliver:
- Summary of highest-leverage distortion
- Most important corrective action
